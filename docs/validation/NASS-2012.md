# NASS 2012 yield vs engine 2012 water deficit (validation)

Source: USDA NASS Quick Stats API (county survey yield, BU / ACRE), pulled 2026-11-15 by `data_pipeline/nass_yield_validation.py`
→ `/srv/hackathon/data/processed/v1/validation/nass_yield.csv` (not committed). Analysis: `data_pipeline/nass_2012_vs_engine.py`;
figure `docs/validation/nass_2012_vs_engine.png`; table `docs/validation/nass_2012_table.csv`.
Baseline = mean of 2001–2011 + 2013–2024 (2025 excluded; Union soybeans has one missing year, n=22; others n=23).
Engine deficit = 2012 season water deficit, strategy A, mean over the project's sampled fields per county (`engine_2012_deficit.csv` + `fields_20.csv`).

| County | Crop | 2012 yield (bu/ac) | Baseline (bu/ac) | Change | Engine deficit (mm) | Fields |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Story IA | corn | 157.6 | 181.2 | −13.0 % | 469.9 | 12 |
| Story IA | soy | 48.9 | 52.0 | −5.9 % | 469.9 | 12 |
| McLean IL | corn | 109.5 | 195.6 | −44.0 % | 414.8 | 3 |
| McLean IL | soy | 52.3 | 59.4 | −11.9 % | 414.8 | 3 |
| Lancaster NE | corn | 81.3 | 141.6 | −42.6 % | 643.3 | 3 |
| Lancaster NE | soy | 25.0 | 44.4 | −43.7 % | 643.3 | 3 |
| Union OH | corn | 135.5 | 165.4 | −18.1 % | 306.5 | 2 |
| Union OH | soy | 44.4 | 48.7 | −8.8 % | 306.5 | 2 |

Correlation (deficit vs yield change, across counties): corn r = −0.49, soy r = −0.85, **n = 4 counties per crop**.

**Caveat (plain):** n = 4 is far too small for inference; neither correlation is statistically meaningful (soy r = −0.85 at n = 4 gives p ≈ 0.15),
the corn relationship is weak (McLean had a −44 % corn year with a mid-range deficit), and the engine uses 2–12 fields per county, not the whole county.
Yield is not the engine objective (the engine models water deficit and stress, not yield). This is a directional sanity check only.

## Paste-ready paragraph (S5 panel / slides / Details)

> Sanity check against USDA NASS county yields (Quick Stats API, `nass_yield.csv`): in the 2012 drought all four study counties
> (Story IA, McLean IL, Lancaster NE, Union OH) had below-baseline yields, corn −13 % to −44 % and soybeans −6 % to −44 % versus each county's
> 2001–2024 mean excluding 2012. The county with the largest engine water deficit (Lancaster NE, 643 mm) also had the largest soybean loss (−44 %), and the
> smallest deficit (Union OH, 307 mm) had a moderate corn loss (−18 %) and the smallest soybean loss after Story. Across counties the direction agrees
> (more deficit, bigger loss; r = −0.49 corn, −0.85 soybeans), but with only four counties this is directional, not statistical, evidence. Yield is not the engine's objective and
> the engine's field samples do not cover whole counties.
